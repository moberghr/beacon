### sales.order_lines
  One line per ordered item
  Columns:
    - (order_id: integer, PK, NOT NULL, Order the line belongs to)
    - (tenant_id: integer, PK, NOT NULL)
    - (line_no: smallint, PK, NOT NULL)
    - (sku: varchar(32), NOT NULL, Examples: [A-1, B-2])
    - (status: text, NOT NULL, Line state, Values (all 3): [open, shipped, returned])
    - (amount: numeric(12,2))
    - (contact_email: varchar(255), Buyer contact)
    - (notes: text)
  Foreign Keys:
    - order_id → sales.orders.id
    - tenant_id → sales.orders.tenant_id

### sales.orders

    - customer_id → crm.customers.id
